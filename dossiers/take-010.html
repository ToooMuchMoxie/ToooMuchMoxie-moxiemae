Moxie Mae’s Take #010 — Dossier

Klan Hoods in the Tournament Doc

The University of Maryland's club tennis team emailed visiting teams a tournament doc with KKK hoods drawn into the state flag — on page one, under their own logo.

The Claim

For its Fall Invitational (Oct 10–11, 2026), UMD club tennis emailed a Google Doc to participating teams. Directly beneath the Maryland Club Tennis logo, the Maryland flag's Crossland quadrants were replaced with Klan hood masks, eye holes and all. Howard University's club caught it the night before the tournament.

The Receipts

Receipt #1 — The image

Hoods placed in two specific flag quadrants, under the club logo, on page one of a doc emailed to visiting teams — including an HBCU. Somebody didn't stumble into that. Somebody built it.

Source: Howard club's public statement and the photo shared with the Washington Post (Take #010 verified story)

Receipt #2 — Who caught it

Howard club president Toni Rachal, who opened the doc Friday evening: “I wish I could say that this was surprising, but I've played tennis my whole entire life, and unfortunately, this behavior is very normal.”

Source: Toni Rachal to the Washington Post (Take #010 verified story)

Receipt #3 — The fallout

UMD said it was “horrified” and apologized; the ENTIRE club was suspended indefinitely, matches forfeited, the matter referred to campus police, Student Conduct, and Civil Rights offices. Howard's demands include a public apology, a real inquiry, and tournament proceeds donated to the National Museum of African American History and Culture.

Source: AP; NBC News; The Diamondback; Washington Post (Take #010 sources)

Mae’s Verdict“A Klan hood doesn't land in a Google Doc by accident, Maryland.” Who created the graphic was not publicly identified when the Take aired, and the Take named no individual students.
