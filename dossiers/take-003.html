Moxie Mae’s Take #003 — Dossier

The Da'Vinchi Blackout

BMF star Da'Vinchi sat down with Cam Newton and called Americans “incompetent” and “lazy” — then the audience answered with leverage.

The Claim

On Cam Newton's Funky Friday podcast, Da'Vinchi (Abraham Juste) said Americans have been “incompetent for a really long time,” doubled down on “very lazy and poor” work ethic, and agreed it is “true” that some in the Caribbean, Africa and the UK think they are better than Black Americans — then pivoted to “Black people enslaved Black people too.”

The Receipts

Receipt #1 — The clip itself

The Funky Friday exchange, in his own words: “incompetent for a really long time,” the double-down when Cam asked if he meant lazy, and “I think that's true” on the superiority stigma. He also added “I don't think it's right” — credit where it's due, and Mae gives it.

Source: Funky Friday podcast with Cam Newton (aired ~Oct 2, 2026), as researched in the Take #003 file

Receipt #2 — The fallout

Roughly 200,000 Instagram followers lost, and Freedmen Nation added him to its boycott list as the Blackout conversation expanded.

Source: Take #003 verified-facts research (2026-10-11): follower-loss coverage and the Freedmen Nation boycott-list announcement

Mae’s Verdict“The Blackout ain't about hate, it's about leverage. Disrespect the people who made you, and the people stop showing up.”
