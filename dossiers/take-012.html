The Pivot — The FBA Question Talarico Wouldn't Answer

Texas Senate nominee James Talarico got asked the FBA question by a descendant of slavery — and answered a different one.

The Claim

A Texas questioner — a native, a descendant of slavery — asked why money isn't put into the Black neighborhoods that built what immigrants now enjoy, and what Talarico would do to lend a hand. His first move was “we have Black immigrants.” Pressed — “HOW? How?” — the answer was universal pre-K for every child, “no matter their race.”

The Receipts

Receipt #1 — The exchange, verbatim

Questioner: “Let's cut out the Black immigrants right now. I'm talking about Foundational Black Americans… I'm a native of Texas and a descendant of slavery.” Talarico's concrete answer: “high-quality universal early childhood education for every child, no matter their zip code, NO MATTER THEIR RACE, no matter how much money their parents make.”

Source: Verbatim from the campaign-forum clip (Tariq Nasheed's video, transcribed in the Take #012 file)

Receipt #2 — DeSoto, on reparations

July 20, 2026 town hall, verbatim: “Restoration, repairing past harms is part of my faith tradition… Whether it's tuition relief, whether it's home ownership grants, whether it is baby bonds… We have to correct the injustices of our past.” Warm words — but no explicit endorsement of reparations payments and nothing lineage-based.

Source: DeSoto town hall, July 20, 2026 (Take #012 file)

Receipt #3 — The $25 million

At the July Houston town hall he pledged $25M to reach Black voters. Organizers later reported getting “one or 2% of what we were told we were going to get. And then it's been silence since.” His Oct 5 CT-AACES event — 325 confirmed registrants — was canceled during his illness absence with no rescheduled date; organizers cited “irrecoverable financial loss.” Nurse Kandice Webber had confronted him in Houston: “You cannot win without us, so you stay here and you answer our questions, or you suffer the consequences.”

Source: Texas Tribune reporting (via Fox News) and CT-AACES email via Fox News Digital (Take #012 file)

Receipt #4 — His own tradition's receipt

Zacchaeus, Luke 19:8: “Half of my goods I give to the poor. And if I have defrauded anyone of anything, I restore it fourfold.” In Talarico's own faith tradition, restoration came with repayment attached — not programs and pivots.

Source: Luke 19:8 (Take #012 “Mae's Bible receipt”)

Mae’s Verdict“The question had a NAME on it. Universal is what they say when the question had a name on it.” Talarico needs Black Texas turnout in a toss-up Senate race — and the FBA question is still waiting for an FBA answer.
