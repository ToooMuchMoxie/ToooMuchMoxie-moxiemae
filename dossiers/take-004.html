Moxie Mae’s Take #004 — Dossier

Egypt Is in Africa

A viral clip claimed Black people don't come from Africa, we come from Egypt. Her instinct wasn't wrong — her geography was.

The Claim

Egypt is IN Africa. That's like saying you don't come from America, you come from Texas. And the receipts show the ancient Egyptians' own record points the same direction Mae does.

The Receipts

Receipt #1 — Kemet

The Egyptians called their own country Kemet — the Black Land (the black Nile soil, against Deshret, the Red Land desert).

Source: Take #004 verified facts (researched 2026-10-11)

Receipt #2 — Herodotus

Herodotus, Histories 2.104, described the Egyptians as “black-skinned and woolly-haired” — about 2,400 years ago.

Source: Herodotus, Histories 2.104

Receipt #3 — Diop at UNESCO

Cheikh Anta Diop, the Senegalese physicist, melanin-tested mummy skin and presented his evidence at UNESCO's 1974 Cairo symposium on the peopling of ancient Egypt.

Source: Take #004 verified facts; UNESCO Cairo symposium, 1974

Receipt #4 — Real talk

TikTok flagged the viral clip itself as AI-generated media — the woman in it may not even be real, and the clip is built to start fights in the comments.

Source: TikTok's own “Contains AI-generated media” label on the clip, noted in the Take #004 file
