Moxie Mae’s Take #011 — Dossier

Pull the Paperwork

Ilhan Omar, the raid, the one digit her citizenship story turns on, and Minnesota's fraud billions. Mae's rule: documents, dates, and dollar signs — and positions stated as positions, allegations as allegations.

The Claim

On Oct 12, 2026, HSI agents searched a sitting congresswoman's Washington home under a judicial warrant as the DOJ probed her family's immigration paperwork. No charges were filed. Mae's position is conditional and on the law: if the papers are clean, publish them and end it; if citizenship was built on fraud, the statute is the statute — the same as for anybody else.

The Receipts

Receipt #1 — The raid

October 12, 2026 — Homeland Security agents entered Omar's Washington home with a court-authorized warrant and left with boxes; the DOJ is examining her family's immigration paperwork. A warrant is not a verdict, and the Take says so.

Source: Take #011 research file (2026-10-14); no charges filed

Receipt #2 — One digit

Omar's citizenship derived through her father's 2000 naturalization, when she was a minor. Congressional records say born 1982 — seventeen in 2000. Minnesota's Legislative Reference Library listed 1981 for years — eighteen in 2000, too old for that pathway — and her staff had it revised to 1982 after she took office; the library says the 1981 listing came from Wikipedia “without documentation.”

Source: Take #011 research file: Minnesota Legislative Reference Library record history

Receipt #3 — Minnesota money

Feeding Our Future: ~$250 million stolen from a federal child-nutrition program — the largest pandemic-relief fraud charged in U.S. history. Autism-therapy claims in one MN program: ~$1M (2017) to $220M+ (2024). Housing Stabilization: $302M paid vs ~$12M expected, prosecutors say mostly fraudulent. Prosecutors' accounting across the schemes: of 86 charged, all but eight are of Somali ancestry; 59+ convicted by late 2025.

Source: Federal prosecutors' numbers via AP/DOJ reporting (Take #011 research file)

Receipt #4 — The speech — both translations on the record

Jan 27, 2024, Minneapolis: Omar addressed a Somali crowd in Somali about Somalia/Somaliland politics. The viral translation rendered “Somalians first” and America following Somali orders; the Star Tribune and Minnesota Reformer retranslated and called the viral version distorted. Mae's point stands on either version: no version of that speech was about Black America.

Source: Star Tribune; Minnesota Reformer; viral translation via Somaliland's deputy foreign minister (Take #011 research file)

Show the paperwork… or keep the questions.” The decade-old “brother” claim is carried in the file as what it is: investigated for ten years, with zero documentary proof — Mae doesn't print what ain't proven.
