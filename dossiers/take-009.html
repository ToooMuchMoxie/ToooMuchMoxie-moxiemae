Moxie Mae’s Take #009 — Dossier

They Kept Score

A Charlotte-Mecklenburg police group chat surfaced — and Mae labels every receipt confirmed or sourced, because that's how receipts work.

The Claim

A group text thread among CMPD officers surfaced from a charged officer's downloaded phone: per WBTV's sources, a contest over who could pull over the most Black drivers, the n-word tied to talk of force and shooting Black residents, and a thread comparing the crew to a Klan rally. Six officers were suspended without pay and cited for termination; a seventh resigned.

The Receipts

Receipt #1 — The scoreboard — SOURCED, not CMPD-confirmed

WBTV's anonymous sources describe the contest and the force/shooting talk. CMPD has not publicly confirmed the specific messages, and Charlotte Focal flags the competition claim as unconfirmed. Mae attributes it to WBTV's sources — exactly as the Take did on air.

Source: WBTV Investigates, Oct 12–13, 2026; Charlotte Focal caveat (Take #009 receipts discipline)

Receipt #2 — How it surfaced — CONFIRMED

Officer Seth Elliott was charged in July 2026 with illegally accessing a government computer in an SBI probe of alleged Flock plate-reader misuse. He resigned; the state downloaded his phone, and the chat fell out. No whistleblower — their own group chat.

Source: WBTV / WCNC / WSOC reporting (Take #009 verified story)

Receipt #3 — The fallout — CONFIRMED

Six officers suspended WITHOUT PAY, cited for termination (Civil Service Board hearings; Chief Estella Patterson has final say). A seventh officer resigned in August, connected per sources. Activists and Councilman Malcolm Graham called for scrutiny of the officers' past stops and arrests.

Source: WSOC-TV; WCNC Charlotte; The Root (Take #009 verified story)

Mae’s Verdict“They kept score on Black bodies — and got caught by their own group chat.” The six officers' names were not public and the Take named only Elliott, the officer charged publicly.
