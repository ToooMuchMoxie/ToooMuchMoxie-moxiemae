Moxie Mae’s Take #005 — Dossier

Egypt Was in America?

After Take #004, the comments filled with “Egypt was in America” — the Grand Canyon is Egypt, the Mississippi is the Nile. Mae checked.

The Claim

The Grand Canyon's Egyptian names are real. The Egyptian civilization in the Grand Canyon is not. A name is not a civilization — and the famous “Egyptian cave” is a 1909 newspaper hoax.

The Receipts

Receipt #1 — The names

Isis Temple, Cheops Pyramid, Tower of Ra are real canyon landmarks — named by geologist Clarence Dutton in his 1882 survey, after gods and legends from every culture: the canyon also has Vishnu Temple, Apollo Temple, Shiva Temple and a King Arthur castle.

Source: Take #005 verified facts (researched 2026-10-12)

Receipt #2 — The cave that never was

On April 5, 1909, the Arizona Gazette claimed Smithsonian explorers G.E. Kinkaid and Prof. S.A. Jordan found mummies in a canyon citadel. The Smithsonian says those men never existed in its records — and in 100+ years, not one Egyptian artifact has ever been found in the Grand Canyon.

Source: Smithsonian Institution position, as compiled in the Take #005 file; Arizona Gazette, Apr 5, 1909

Receipt #3 — Two rivers, two continents

The Nile runs about 4,130 miles through Africa into the Mediterranean. The Mississippi runs about 2,320 miles — a great American river that is simply not that one.

Source: Take #005 verified facts
