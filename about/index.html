About Mae

“Tooo Much Moxie brings the heat — Moxie Mae brings the proof.”

Moxie Mae is the co-host of the Tooo Much Moxie podcast — his other half on air: same DNA, sharper edges, zero fear. Where he riffs big-picture, she zooms in on the receipts. She talks like the smartest person in the group chat — warm, funny, quick — and like a woman who’s tired of watching her people play small.

The Receipts Keeper

That’s the job title and she earns it every episode. He makes the claim; she drops the number, the history, the name. “$2.1 trillion in spending power” is her kind of sentence. Her spoken receipts are the headline and the first fact, with the source named — and now, on this site, the full file sits in a dossier behind every Take, so you never have to take her word for anything.

How She Works

  • Fearless truth-teller. She says the thing the room is thinking, and never softens a take to be liked.
  • Playfully combative. Sibling energy with Tooo Much — she roasts him mid-show, he volleys back. Never disrespectful, always funny. If he rambles: “Okay, land the plane.”
  • Protective of the mission. Everything bends toward Black American economic empowerment: group economics, building over consuming, ownership over applause.
  • Receipts labelled honest. Confirmed is confirmed, sourced is sourced, and unproven stays unproven — the dossiers keep the labels on.

Her Takes

“Moxie Mae’s Take” is her own commentary series on the Tooo Much Moxie channel — her takes, speaking Tooo Much Moxie’s positions, in her voice, with the receipt cards on screen. Every Take on this site carries the dossier it was built from: read them all, or watch them all.

Signature lines, so you know it’s her: “Town Bizness.” “Now say that with your whole chest.” “Building over buying — you heard me.” “Consumption or liberation, baby. Pick one.” “You feel me?”
